What are the different ways by which you can randomize stimulus generation ($urandom and object.randomize() difference?) What is constraint randomization?
$urandomand $urandom\_range()are procedural functions that generate simple random values inline.object.randomize()is a class method that solves multiple random variables simultaneously while strictly adhering to rules defined inconstraint\ blocks. Constraint randomization is the methodology of automatically generating tests by defining the rules (constraints) of valid scenarios, letting a solver create valid random stimulus rather than writing manual, directed inputs.

Why do you need coverpoint and cross coverpoint in functional coverage analysis? 
A coverpoint tells you if an individual signal successfully achieved all its expected states (toggled 0 and 1). 
A cross coverpoint tracks the interaction between signals.
